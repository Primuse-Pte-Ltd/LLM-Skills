VENUE INTERIOR 4:5

The Stage is not merely a venue — it is a multi-space, multifunctional destination where the extraordinary becomes reality. Nestled in the heart of Seminyak, Bali, we have crafted Asia's most exclusive multifunctional luxury venue — a single destination that hosts world-class concerts, curated cultural events, corporate hire, weddings, private celebrations, and exclusive nightclub nights under one roof.

From world-renowned concerts to intimate comedy nights, from electrifying club evenings to sophisticated corporate galas and elegant weddings, The Stage transforms every event into an unforgettable moment. Every booking is tailored to the guest: bespoke table service, personal concierge, custom menus, and dedicated production support. Frequently Asked

About The Stage Bali

Everything you need to know about visiting Bali's premier luxury nightclub and concert venue, from opening hours in Seminyak to VIP table reservations and private event hire.

What is The Stage Bali?

The Stage Bali is a premier multi-space, multifunctional luxury venue in Seminyak. Under one roof it operates as a concert hall for international artists, a private events space for weddings, corporate hire, and brand activations, and a club and lounge floor for exclusive nightlife.

Where is The Stage Bali located?

The Stage Bali is located at Jl. Kayu Aya No.7, Seminyak, Kuta Utara, Badung, Bali 80361, Indonesia — minutes from Seminyak Beach and Petitenget.

What kinds of events does The Stage Bali host?

The Stage Bali hosts world-class concerts and live-music showcases, corporate galas, weddings, product launches, brand activations, comedy nights, and exclusive club evenings. As a multifunctional venue, each space is configured to match the event.

Can I hire The Stage Bali for a private or corporate event?

Yes. The Stage Bali's private events space is available for weddings, corporate parties, gala dinners, product launches, and brand activations, with dedicated event production and custom F&B. Contact our VIP concierge at vip@thestage.net for bespoke event planning and exclusive venue-hire inquiries.

How do I book a VIP table or luxury sofa at The Stage Bali?

You can reserve VIP tables and luxury sofa packages online through the Reserve VIP page, or contact our concierge on WhatsApp at +62 811 3831 4177. Every VIP reservation includes dedicated table service, priority entry, and a personal host.

What are The Stage Bali's opening hours?

The club and lounge floor opens Thursday, Friday, and Saturday from 10:00 PM to 4:00 AM local time (WITA). Concert and private-event hours vary by booking — check the events schedule or contact our team.

Is there a dress code at The Stage Bali?

Yes — smart elegant attire is required. Sportswear, flip-flops, and casual beach wear are not permitted. Management reserves the right to refuse entry.
