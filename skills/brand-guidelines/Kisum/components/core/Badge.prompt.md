One-line: Small pill for counts, status, and metadata.

```jsx
<Badge tone="brand">12</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" dot>Confirmed</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">Overdue</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" uppercase>Concert</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | brand | success | danger | warning | outline`. `dot` adds a leading status dot; `pill` (default true) toggles full round; `uppercase` for tag-style chips.
