The Purple Budget Rule

Kisum purple on ≤10% of any screen — ranks, badges, one primary CTA. Everywhere = hierarchy collapse.

The No-Cream Rule

Neutrals stay cool (zinc/gray). Never tint the page warm. Warmth comes from artist imagery, not the canvas.

The Flat-By-Default Rule

Surfaces are flat at rest. Depth from borders + tonal layering. Shadows signal interactivity only.

The Density Rule

Prefer 14px body on analyst surfaces. Step up only for a hero name or primary KPI.
