# The Stage

The Stage brand: a luxury event venue in Bali. Deep forest greens, a single muted gold accent, and a light, wide display serif.

This is a quick reference for the brand. The source of truth is `dirsal-brands-guidelines` → `Projects/TheStage/README.md` and its `styles.css`. Use those files for anything beyond color and type.

## Color Palette

- **Dark Green**: `#0a140f` - Page background
- **Moss**: `#243d2f` - Raised panels and cards on the dark page
- **Muted Gold**: `#c9a962` - The single accent: primary buttons and highlights (hover `#d4b978`)
- **Cream**: `#f5f3ee` - Body text on dark, and light panels
- **Champagne**: `#d4c8b8` - Secondary text (muted text `#9a998f`)

## Typography

- **Headers**: Cormorant Garamond (normal weight, wide tracking, never bold)
- **Body Text**: Montserrat
- **Small labels**: Montserrat, extra small, uppercase, widest tracking, in `#9a998f`. This is the venue's signature

## Best Used For

Anything for The Stage: event and NYE announcements, venue-hire and private-event proposals, VIP table menus, sponsor decks, and website pages. Use the white logo from `Projects/TheStage/templates/*/assets/logoW.svg`.
