# Nextkt

The Nextkt brand: the standalone ticketing platform (storefront, operator admin, sysadmin console, developer portal). A clean light surface with teal and navy taken from the logo.

**Provisional.** Nextkt's design-system files are not in `dirsal-brands-guidelines` yet (`Projects/Nextkt/README.md` is a placeholder). These values come from the live storefront code, `Nextkt-Frontend/tailwind.config.ts`, and are replaced when the design system arrives. Say that when you apply them. Inside a Nextkt repo, that repo's current tokens win over this file.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Teal**: `#1c6e87` - Primary buttons, links, and active states
- **Steel Blue**: `#417292` - Secondary accent and general-admission tier
- **Deep Navy**: `#1d1e4c` - Headings and body text
- **Surface**: `#f8f9fa` - Page background (cards `#ffffff`, borders `#c3c7cc`)
- **VIP Gold**: `#d4af37` - VIP tier only, never a general accent

The logo itself uses teal `#488790`, steel blue `#417292`, and deep navy `#1d1e4c`.

## Typography

- **Headers**: Inter (600–700, slight negative tracking on large sizes)
- **Body Text**: Inter

## Best Used For

Anything for Nextkt: event storefront pages, ticket and checkout flows, operator and box-office reports, developer-portal docs, and Nextkt product decks. Use the logo from the Nextkt repo (`public/images/logo.svg`); never redraw it.
