# Nextkt

The Nextkt brand: a calm, light ticketing storefront ("House Lights Down") that opens onto photo-first, glassy stage moments. Teal is the only action colour, on navy ink and cool neutrals.

This is a quick reference for the brand. The source of truth is `dirsal-brands-guidelines` → `Projects/Nextkt/` (`readme.md`, `tokens/`, `styles.css`, `uploads/DESIGN.md`). Use those files for anything beyond color and type. Inside a Nextkt repo, that repo's current tokens win over this file.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Teal**: `#1c6e87` - CTAs, links, active nav, prices, eyebrows, focus ring. Action only, never decorative
- **Steel Blue**: `#417292` - Teal hover, the Add to Cart fill, and the general-admission tier
- **Deep Navy**: `#1d1e4c` - Headings and body text
- **Surface**: `#f8f9fa` - Page background (cards `#ffffff`, hairlines `#c3c7cc`, never cream)
- **VIP Gold**: `#d4af37` - VIP tier only, never a general accent

The logo lockup ("NexTicket") uses steel `#417292`, teal `#488790`, and deep navy `#1d1e4c`; the standalone N mark is `#3e6f8f`. The storefront is light-only; dark ("Dark Editorial", base `#191c1d`) is for scoped featured bands.

## Typography

- **Headers**: Inter (display 48/700, headlines 32 and 24 at 600, −0.02em on large sizes)
- **Body Text**: Inter (18/16; labels 12/700 uppercase, 0.05em tracking)

Radii are small: buttons 4px, cards 8px, pills 12px, modals 16px.

## Best Used For

Anything for Nextkt: event storefront pages, ticket and checkout flows, operator and box-office reports, developer-portal docs, and Nextkt product decks. Use the logo SVGs from `Projects/Nextkt/assets/`; never redraw or recolor them.
