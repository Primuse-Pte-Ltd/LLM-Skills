# Kisum

The Kisum brand: an analyst-confident, information-dense editorial look for the live-music operating platform. Cool neutrals with a single purple accent.

This is a quick reference for the brand. The source of truth is `dirsal-brands-guidelines` → `Projects/Kisum/` (`readme.md`, `tokens/`, `styles.css`). Use those files for anything beyond color and type.

## Color Palette

- **Kisum Purple**: `#8466AC` - The only accent: ranks, count badges, one primary CTA per view, active nav. Keep it to 10% of any screen or slide
- **Ink**: `#18181B` - Headings and body text
- **Muted**: `#71717A` - Secondary text and labels
- **Page**: `#F9FAFB` - Page background (cool, never cream)
- **Card**: `#FFFFFF` - Cards and panels, separated by `#E5E7EB` 1px borders

Dark mode: canvas `#0E0E11`, cards `#1E1E24`, text `#E4E1E9`. Purple fills stay `#8466AC`; purple text lifts to `#CDB4F0`.

## Typography

- **Headers**: Manrope (600–800, tight tracking)
- **Body Text**: Inter (14px default, tabular numerals for data)

## Best Used For

Anything for Kisum: booking briefs, promoter and artist reports, rankings, finance summaries, product decks, and Kisum marketing pages. Use the logo SVGs from `Projects/Kisum/assets/`; never redraw them.
