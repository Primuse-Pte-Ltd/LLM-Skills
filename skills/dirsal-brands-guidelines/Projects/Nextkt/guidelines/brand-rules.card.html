Teal-Is-Action

#1C6E87 marks what you can do: primary CTAs, links, active nav, the cart badge, the focus ring. Steel #417292 is only its hover. Never decorate with teal.

Gold-Is-Earned

#D4AF37 belongs to the VIP tier and loyalty rewards only. Never on buttons, links or placeholders. Gold everywhere cheapens VIP.

Photo-First

The chrome stays quiet: navy ink on #F8F9FA, white 8px cards, hairline #C3C7CC borders. Artist photos provide the drama, under a black/80 scrim with glass chips.

Two-Layouts

Desktop and mobile are separate compositions split at lg. Desktop gets a sticky 96px header and grids; mobile gets a 60px app bar, a bottom tab bar and stacked rows. Don't just reflow one into the other.
