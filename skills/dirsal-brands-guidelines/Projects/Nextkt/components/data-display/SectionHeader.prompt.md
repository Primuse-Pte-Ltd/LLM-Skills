One-line: section title row — desktop headline + underlined teal link, mobile headline + caps "See All", or a caps eyebrow label.

```jsx
<SectionHeader title="Upcoming Events" subtitle="Discover live experiences happening near you soon." action="View Full Calendar" />
<SectionHeader size="md" title="Premiere Events" action="See All" />
<SectionHeader size="eyebrow" title="You might also like" action="View All" />
```

Section titles are short Title Case nouns ("Premiere Events", "About the Event", "Order Summary"). Leave `stack-lg` (32px) below a desktop header before the grid.
