One-line: mobile upcoming-events row — date leaf, title, venue, teal price, heart.

```jsx
<EventRow month="NOV" day="02" title="Velvet Jazz Nights" venue="Motion Blue" city="Jakarta" price="From Rp 350.000" />
<EventRow month="NOV" day="15" title="Comedy Underground" venue="Kuningan City" city="Jakarta" price="From Rp 200.000" last />
```

Rows are separated by a bottom `outline-variant` hairline (no card box). The heart turns filled red (`--error`) when active — the only place red marks a positive action.
